# Phase 3 Intelligence Audit: Quantitative Analysis & Behavioral Segmentation

## 1. Most Recurring Barriers
The extraction identified 476 relevant shopping observations across four sources.
- **UX/Other Shopping Friction**: 26 occurrences across 4 sources. Strength: Strong
- **Price/Value Hesitation**: 17 occurrences across 3 sources. Strength: Strong
- **Fit/Quality/Size Uncertainty**: 12 occurrences across 4 sources. Strength: Strong
- **Availability/Fulfillment Constraints**: 6 occurrences across 2 sources. Strength: Moderate
- **Return/Exchange Friction**: 6 occurrences across 3 sources. Strength: Strong

## 2. Direct vs Indirect Wishlist Connection
- **Direct Wishlist Evidence**: 6 records explicitly tied to saving or wishlist behaviors.
- **General Shopping Evidence**: 470 records detailing friction without explicit wishlist linkage.

## 3. Co-occurrences
These combinations appear natively in the Phase 2 dataset:
- **Wishlist + Availability/Fulfillment**: 4 occurrences
- **Wishlist + UX**: 1 occurrences

## 4. Cross-Source Prevalence
- **YouTube** dominates in fit, size, and styling queries.
- **Myntra Web** focuses on product evaluation (material).
- **App Stores** dominate in post-cart friction (return policies, pincode unserviceable, delivery fees).

## 5. Behavioral Evidence Profiles
- **Wishlist Holders**: 5 records
- **Price-Hesitant Shoppers**: 19 records
- **Fit/Quality Uncertain Shoppers**: 12 records
- **Availability-Constrained Shoppers**: 6 records

## 6. Contradictions
- No significant contradictory evidence was found (e.g., users claiming prices are excellent vs. users complaining about prices). Complaints were largely unified around specific hidden fees (convenience fee) rather than base pricing.

## 7. Evidence Gaps: What the dataset CANNOT establish
- Actual wishlist → purchase conversion rates.
- 30-day conversion tracking.
- Wishlist item age or time-to-purchase.
- Purchase vs abandonment ultimate outcomes (most complain about barriers, but outcome is unknown).
- User-level journey mapping.
- Item-level conversion statistics.
- Definitively proving whether a specific barrier caused abandonment, unless explicitly stated.
